## Maximum-degree theorems (2026-09-24)

One theorem, `GridGen.Polymer.CertifiedAt` (`GridGen/PolymerCertificate.lean`), gives ECC, strict
inequality when adjacent lists differ, and the equality case on connected graphs from a rational
`ParityCert`. Instances: `567Δ ≤ 100k` (all graphs), `Δ ≤ 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8` at
`k ≥ 15, 20, 26, 32, 37, 43`, so every rectangle at `k ≥ 20`. `lake build GridGen.PolymerAxiomAudit`
runs 28 guarded checks. The limiting constant 5.664Δ is Dong–Koh's (2008) real-chromatic-root
bound; credit for the positivity half is theirs. Argument, table and credit:
[`GridGen/README.md`](../GridGen/README.md). The dated notes it replaces are in `gridgen_history/`.
Degree four below 20, or a factor near 5, needs a new idea, not new constants.
