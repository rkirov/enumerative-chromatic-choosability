   height three it reduces ECC to two linear seam inequalities, `(H1)` and Perron growth `(C1)`;

   30%, the block model is independently confirmed (39 types, 43,737 seams), and two no-go
   results explain why `k = 3` needs the entropy certificate. Tools in `cx/eig/`.

## Scripts

- `grid_3xn_*.py` — the height-three certificate and its probes. **Live**: rerunning
  `grid_3xn_dinitz_parry_exact_certificate.py` is one of the two open gates.
- `ladder_all_k_verify.py` — checks the ladder note. Superseded in force by the Lean proof, kept
  as an independent numerical cross-check.
- `cx/eig/` — the tooling of `GRID_HEIGHT3_EIGENFUNCTIONAL`: exact sweeps, chain analysers,
  lookback testers, the independent block model, and the LP/fixed-point certificate tests. Its
  `README.md` gives the command behind every table in that note.
- `cx/eig/gen5.py`, `cx/eig/gen6.py`, `cx/eig/relax.py` — the generators of the `k = 4` certificate
  modules: `gen6.py all4` writes `Grid3/Four/ArithPair/P*.lean` (one theorem per module, only the
  `pX, pZ` bits case-split, McCormick product facts for the rest); `gen5.py glue` writes
  `Expand.lean`, `ExpandSingle.lean`, and `ArithSingle.lean`, while its `pair`, `single`, and
  `master` modes write the set-theoretic glue and dispatcher; `relax.py` is the LP experiment that
  showed which bits need a split. The exact regeneration sequence is in `cx/eig/README.md`.
- `cx/` — route-testing tooling for `GRID_PROOF_ROUTES` and `Q1_COUNTEREXAMPLE_SEARCH`.
  `cx/h3/` is the height-three work of `GRID_HEIGHT3_OBSTRUCTION` (closed form, deficiency
  classification, the certificate-class refutations, exact `Z[rho]` screening, beam searches).
